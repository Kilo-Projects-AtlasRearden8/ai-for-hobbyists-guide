# Running Free AI Models Locally

You can run free AI models on your own computer. Nothing you type leaves your machine, and you do not need an account or an internet connection after setup. This is the most private way to use AI.

**Remember:** Never paste personal info into an AI tool, even one running locally. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## Why run AI locally

- Your chats stay on your computer
- No account, no email, no subscription
- Works offline once the model is downloaded
- Good for sensitive hobby notes (collection lists, project plans, budgets)

## Option A: Ollama (simplest)

Ollama is a free program that downloads and runs AI models for you.

1. Go to ollama.com and download Ollama for your operating system (Windows, Mac, or Linux).
2. Install it like any other program.
3. Open a terminal or command prompt and run:
   `ollama run llama3.2`
   (The first time, it downloads the model. This may take a few minutes.)
4. Type your question and press Enter.
5. Type `/bye` to exit, or just close the window.

Try other small, free models:
- `ollama run gemma3`
- `ollama run phi4`

## Option B: LM Studio (visual, no terminal)

LM Studio lets you browse and run models with a mouse, no terminal required.

1. Go to lmstudio.ai and download the free app.
2. Install and open it.
3. Use the built-in model browser to download a small model (for example, Llama 3.2 or Gemma).
4. Click the model and start a chat in the app window.

## Choosing your first model

| Model | Size | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Llama 3.2 (1B or 3B) | Very small | Fast, simple chats on older computers |
| Gemma 3 | Small | Good general beginner model |
| Phi-4 | Small | Strong reasoning for its size |

If your computer has 8 GB of RAM or less, start with a 1B or 3B model. Larger models need more memory and run slower.

## What local AI is good for

- Drafting project plans and supply lists
- Explaining craft or game rules
- Writing emails and listings
- Summarizing notes you already wrote

## What local AI is not good for

- Looking up current prices or store stock
- Remembering past conversations after you close it (unless you save them)
- Replacing hands-on practice or professional advice

## Safety

Local AI keeps your data on your machine, but it does not make the AI trustworthy. Always double-check facts, especially prices, measurements, and safety instructions. For electrical, plumbing, or structural work, call a licensed professional.
