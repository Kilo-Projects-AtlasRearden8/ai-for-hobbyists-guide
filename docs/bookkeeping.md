# Bookkeeping Basics with AI

This page shows how to use free AI to understand small-business and hobby bookkeeping. It is educational, not accounting advice. For tax filing, talk to a qualified accountant.

**Remember:** Never paste real bank statements, tax IDs, customer payment info, or your Social Security number into an AI tool. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## What AI can help with

- Explaining bookkeeping terms in plain English (income, expense, profit, receipt, invoice)
- Suggesting categories for your income and expenses
- Creating a simple spreadsheet layout for tracking sales
- Drafting a simple invoice template
- Explaining the difference between a hobby and a business for tax purposes

## What AI cannot do

- File your taxes
- Give you tax or legal advice
- Replace an accountant or bookkeeper
- Guarantee deductions or savings

## A simple income/expense tracker

Ask:

> Suggest a simple spreadsheet layout for tracking hobby sales and expenses. Include columns for date, item, income, expense, category, and notes. Give an example row.

Then build it in Google Sheets, Excel, or LibreOffice Calc (all free).

## Example: invoice template

Ask:

> Write a simple invoice template for a small hobby sale. Include fields for seller name (use [YOUR NAME]), buyer (use [BUYER NAME]), date, item, price, and total. Do not include real personal details.

## Keep records safe

- Save receipts and invoices in a folder (paper or digital)
- Back up your spreadsheet regularly
- Do not store real customer payment data in AI chats

## When to talk to a pro

If you are collecting sales tax, forming an LLC, or filing business taxes, talk to a qualified accountant. AI can explain concepts, but it cannot file for you.
