# Book Marketing for Beginners

This page shows simple, free ways to tell people about a book you published — using AI as a helper, not a replacement for your own voice.

**Remember:** Never paste customer names, email lists, or sales data into an AI tool. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## Free marketing ideas AI can help with

- Drafting social media posts about your book
- Writing an email to friends and family announcing your book
- Suggesting places to share your book (hobby forums, local libraries, book clubs)
- Creating a simple press release template
- Brainstorming keywords and categories

## A safe first step

Ask the AI:

> I published a beginner guide about [hobby]. Write three short social media posts announcing it. Keep each under 60 words, friendly, and not pushy. Use placeholders like [BOOK TITLE] and [LINK] where needed.

Fill in the placeholders yourself before posting.

## What to avoid

- Buying followers or reviews
- Sharing readers' personal information
- Making income claims ("earn $X per month") — these can be misleading and may violate platform rules

## Keep it small

You do not need a marketing campaign. Telling a few people who share your hobby is often enough to start.
