# Low-Energy Productivity (ADHD-Friendly)

This page shows how to use free AI to make everyday tasks easier when you have low energy or executive-function challenges. It is written in a warm, non-judgmental tone. AI is a helper, not a cure.

**Remember:** Never paste personal info, health details, or anyone else's private information into an AI tool. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## Why AI helps with low energy

- It breaks big tasks into tiny steps
- It gives you a starting point so you do not face a blank page
- It can re-explain things as many times as you need, without judgment

## The "too big to start" problem

When a task feels overwhelming, ask:

> Break [task, e.g., "clean the craft room" or "file my taxes"] into steps so small each one takes under five minutes. List them in order. Keep the tone kind and encouraging.

Do one tiny step, then stop if you need to. That counts.

## Body doubling with AI

Ask the AI to be your "body double" — a quiet companion while you work:

> I am going to work on [task] for ten minutes. Check in with me in a friendly, low-pressure way, then leave me alone to work.

You can paste this into a chat, set a timer, and work alongside it.

## Decision fatigue

Ask:

> I need to choose between [option A] and [option B] for [hobby project]. List two pros and two cons for each, in plain language. Do not tell me what to choose — just lay it out.

You decide.

## Energy-friendly habits

- Keep a "done list" instead of only a to-do list. Write down what you finished, no matter how small.
- Use the `docs/project-planner.md` to plan one tiny hobby project at a time.
- Rest is part of the process, not a failure.
