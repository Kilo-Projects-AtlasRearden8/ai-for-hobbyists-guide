# Budgeting with AI

This page shows how to use free AI to build a simple budget. It is educational, not financial advice. For major money decisions, talk to a qualified financial professional.

**Remember:** Never paste real bank account numbers, credit card numbers, or exact transaction details into an AI tool. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## Why budget

A budget helps you see where your money goes so you can spend on what matters — including hobbies — without guesswork.

## A simple budget to start

1. List your monthly income (use round numbers or placeholders like `[MY INCOME]`).
2. List your monthly needs: rent, food, utilities, transportation.
3. List your wants: hobbies, entertainment, dining out.
4. Subtract needs from income. What is left is for wants and savings.

Ask the AI:

> Help me build a simple monthly budget. I have [income] coming in and spend about [rent] on rent, [food] on groceries, and [utilities] on utilities. Suggest how much I could set aside for hobbies and savings. Use round numbers and keep it encouraging.

## Tracking spending

- Keep receipts in one place (see `docs/paperwork-organizer.md`)
- Review your bank statement once a month
- Write down what you spent in a notebook or free spreadsheet

## Safety with money and AI

- Never share account numbers, passwords, or card details with AI.
- Use placeholders instead of real dollar amounts tied to your accounts if you want to be extra careful.
- AI is not a financial advisor. It cannot see your accounts or guarantee results.
