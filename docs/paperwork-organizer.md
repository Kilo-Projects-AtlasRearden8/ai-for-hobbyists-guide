# Paperwork Organizer for Beginners

This page shows how to use free AI to help organize household paperwork — bills, receipts, warranties, manuals, and important documents.

**Remember:** Never paste photos of IDs, tax forms, bank statements, or documents with your Social Security number into an AI tool. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## A simple folder system

Ask the AI:

> Suggest a simple paper or digital folder system for organizing household paperwork. Name the folders and give examples of what goes in each. Keep it low-maintenance.

Example folders:
- House & home (lease, mortgage, utilities)
- Health (insurance cards, appointment summaries — summaries only, no full medical records)
- Finances (bank statements, tax documents — store securely)
- Vehicles (registration, insurance, maintenance receipts)
- Warranties & manuals (appliances, electronics)
- IDs & legal (store these physically in a safe place; do not email or AI-upload them)

## What to keep vs. what to toss

Ask:

> I have a pile of receipts and bills. Which ones should I keep for taxes, and which can I throw away? Keep the answer general — do not ask for my real documents.

## Using AI with paperwork safely

- Type descriptions, not photos: instead of uploading a bill, say "a utility bill for about $100."
- Redact personal details: replace your name, address, and account numbers with `[NAME]`, `[ADDRESS]`, `[ACCOUNT]`.
- Store original documents in a locked drawer or encrypted folder, not in an AI chat.

## When to get help

If you are organizing paperwork for a legal, medical, or financial matter, talk to a qualified professional. AI can suggest systems, but it cannot advise you on your specific situation.
