# Privacy and Safety

**The one rule that comes before everything else:**

> Never paste personal info into an AI tool.

That means no:

- Your full name, home address, phone number, or email address
- Passwords, PINs, or credit card numbers
- Bank account or tax information
- Health information, medical records, or details about your symptoms
- Anyone else's personal information (family, friends, coworkers, patients)
- Photos of IDs, mail, prescriptions, or documents with personal details

Treat every AI conversation as if it were public. Assume the company behind the AI can read it, store it, and use it to train future models. Even "private" chats are not guaranteed to stay private.

## What to do instead

| Instead of... | Try... |
|---|---|
| "I live at 123 Main Street, what craft stores are nearby?" | "I live in a mid-sized town in Indiana. What kinds of craft stores should I look for?" |
| "Here is my medical bill, help me understand it." | "Explain the common parts of a medical bill, like copay, deductible, and coinsurance, using a made-up example." |
| "Write a letter to my boss named Sarah at the clinic." | "Write a template letter asking to reduce my hours. Use [NAME] and [CLINIC] as placeholders." |
| "Analyze this photo of my ID." | "What information is usually printed on a state ID, and why should I protect it?" |

## If you already pasted personal info

Do not panic. Delete the conversation if the tool lets you, avoid pasting anything else, and do not use that account for sensitive topics going forward. You cannot take back what was already sent, but you can stop adding to it.

## Medical disclaimer

This guide may mention healthcare topics. AI is not a doctor or a nurse. Nothing here is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have a health question, talk to a qualified clinician.

## Local AI is the most private option

If you want extra privacy, run free AI models on your own computer (see `getting-started.md`). Nothing leaves your machine, and no account is required.
