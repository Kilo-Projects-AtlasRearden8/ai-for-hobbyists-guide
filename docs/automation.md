# Simple AI Automation for Hobbies

Automation means letting a computer do a repetitive task for you. You can use free AI to plan small automations for your hobby life — without paying for software.

**Remember:** Never paste personal info, passwords, or account credentials into an AI tool. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## What AI can help you automate

- Turning messy notes into a tidy project list
- Drafting emails or messages to sellers and organizers
- Making shopping lists from project plans
- Summarizing long hobby articles into bullet points
- Renaming files in a folder (with a small script from `docs/free-ai-coding.md`)

## What you should NOT automate with AI

- Sending payments or purchases
- Accessing bank, email, or shopping accounts
- Posting personal details about yourself or others
- Anything involving someone else's private information

## A beginner automation example: project notes to checklist

1. Write your project notes however you want — messy is fine.
2. Ask the AI:

   > Turn these notes into a simple checklist with checkboxes. Keep each item under one line.
   [Paste your notes here, with personal details removed]

3. Copy the checklist into your notes app or print it (`docs/project-planner.md`).

## A beginner automation example: email to a seller

Ask:

> Write a short, polite message to a seller asking about [item]. Ask if it is still available and about the condition. Use [YOUR NAME] and [YOUR CITY] as placeholders. Do not include my real name or address.

Then fill in the placeholders yourself before sending.

## Keeping automations small

Start with tasks that take you five minutes or less and do not involve money or personal data. If an automation saves you time, great. If it creates more work than it saves, skip it.
