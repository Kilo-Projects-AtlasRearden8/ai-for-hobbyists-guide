# Reselling with AI (eBay and Amazon)

This page shows how to use free AI to help with reselling items — for example, books, craft supplies, collectibles, or hobby gear — on eBay, Amazon, or local platforms.

**Remember:** Never paste customer names, addresses, order details, or payment info into an AI tool. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## How AI can help

- Writing honest, clear item titles and descriptions
- Suggesting keywords buyers might search for
- Drafting polite messages to buyers
- Organizing your inventory notes
- Calculating profit ideas (you do the math)

## Example: writing a listing

Ask:

> I am selling a used [item] in [general condition, e.g., good, like new]. Write a short, honest eBay title and description. Include what it is, any flaws, and what comes with it. Use placeholders like [YOUR CITY] instead of my real location.

## Example: replying to a buyer

Ask:

> Write a polite, short reply to a buyer who asks if I can combine shipping on two items. Keep it friendly and professional.

## Rules for safe reselling

- Never share your home address in a public listing. Use the platform's messaging system.
- Consider shipping from a post office or store instead of your home.
- Describe condition honestly. Include flaws.
- AI price suggestions are guesses. Check "sold" listings for real prices.
- Do not resell items you do not own or have the right to sell.
