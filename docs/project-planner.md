Print this page (or copy it into a notes app) before you start an AI hobby project. One page per project keeps you focused and keeps personal info out of AI chats.

**Privacy reminder:** Never paste personal info into an AI tool. No names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, passwords, bank details, health information, or photos that identify you or anyone else. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## How to use this planner

1. **Pick one project.** Small is better than big. One dishcloth, one scrapbook page, one game night.
2. **Write your prompts before you open AI.** Fill in section 3 on paper. This stops you from typing personal details into the chat by accident.
3. **Use placeholders.** Write `[YOUR CITY]`, `[MY NAME]`, or `[ITEM]` instead of real info.
4. **Copy useful answers out of the chat.** AI conversations can disappear. Save what works in a notes app, document, or printed page.
5. **Check safety.** If your project involves tools, electricity, water, or heights, read the safety section of the matching hobby guide first.
