# KDP and Book Publishing for Beginners

This page covers using AI to help with Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) and self-publishing. It is written for authors and hobbyists who want to publish a book — not a guarantee of sales or income.

**Remember:** Never paste your manuscript, personal address, tax ID, or banking info into an AI tool. See `docs/privacy.md`.

## What KDP is

KDP lets you publish ebooks and paperbacks on Amazon for free. You upload your book file and cover, set a price, and Amazon sells it. You earn royalties when it sells.

## How AI can help (education only)

- Brainstorming book titles and subtitles
- Creating a chapter outline from your idea
- Writing a book description or blurb
- Suggesting keywords and categories for your book
- Explaining formatting steps in plain language

## What AI cannot do

- Write your whole book well (readers can tell)
- Guarantee sales or bestseller status
- Replace professional editing or cover design
- Handle your taxes or publishing contracts

## Your first step

Ask the AI:

> I want to write a beginner guide about [your hobby]. Suggest a book title, a subtitle, and a simple 8-chapter outline. Keep the tone friendly and plain-English.

## Publishing checklist

- [ ] Write and revise your manuscript
- [ ] Get someone you trust to read it (not an AI)
- [ ] Format the interior (Kindle Create is a free Amazon tool)
- [ ] Create or commission a cover
- [ ] Set up a free KDP account at kdp.amazon.com
- [ ] Upload your files, set your price, and publish

## Safety on KDP

- KDP will ask for tax and banking information to pay you. Enter that only on Amazon's official site, never into an AI chat.
- Do not publish content you do not have the rights to.
