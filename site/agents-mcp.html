AI Agents & MCP

This page explains "agent" and "MCP" in plain English, with no jargon required.

Remember: Never paste personal info into an AI tool or agent. See Privacy and Safety.

What is an AI agent?

Think of an AI assistant as someone who answers questions. Think of an AI agent as someone who can also do things for you — like searching a website, filling out a form, or moving files — with your permission.

Agents are still new. Treat them like a helpful intern: useful, but always check their work before they do anything important.

What is MCP?

MCP stands for Model Context Protocol. It is a free, open standard that lets an AI assistant connect to tools and data sources you control. Think of MCP as a USB port for AI.

A safe first experiment

Do not connect an agent to your bank, email, or medical accounts. Start with read-only, local data:

  1. Set up local AI (see Local AI).
  2. Try a free, open-source MCP server that reads files on your computer only.
  3. Ask the AI to summarize your own project-planner notes — nothing leaves your machine.
