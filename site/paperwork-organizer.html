Paperwork Organizer

Use free AI to help organize household paperwork — bills, receipts, warranties, manuals, and important documents.

Remember: Never paste photos of IDs, tax forms, bank statements, or documents with your Social Security number into an AI tool. See Privacy and Safety.

Example folders

  • House & home (lease, mortgage, utilities)
  • Health (insurance cards, appointment summaries)
  • Finances (bank statements, tax documents — store securely)
  • Vehicles (registration, insurance, maintenance receipts)
  • Warranties & manuals (appliances, electronics)
