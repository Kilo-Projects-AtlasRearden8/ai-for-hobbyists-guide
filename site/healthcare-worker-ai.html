AI for Healthcare Workers

Non-clinical uses of free AI for healthcare workers — education only, never medical advice.

Remember: Never paste patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, diagnoses, photos, or any identifying health information into an AI tool. See Privacy and Safety. HIPAA and workplace privacy rules still apply.

AI must NOT

  • Diagnose or treat any patient
  • Suggest medications, doses, or care plans
  • Interpret lab results or imaging
  • Replace a clinician's assessment
  • Handle real patient data
