AI for Gaming

Tabletop games, video games, and online games — including FFXIV. AI helps you learn rules, build characters, plan strategies, and keep track of game notes. You still do the playing.

Remember: Never paste personal info into an AI tool. See Privacy and Safety. Do not share your game account name, password, server name, character name tied to your real identity, or screenshots that show personal details.

Project 1: Learn a game you just bought

  1. Ask for a beginner summary.
I just started playing [game name]. I have never played anything like it. Explain the basics in plain English: what the goal is, how a turn or round works, and what a typical first hour looks like. Keep it under 300 words.
  1. Ask about one confusing part. Ask: "I do not understand [specific mechanic, e.g., advantage/disadvantage, mana, aggro]. Explain it with a simple example."
  2. Get a cheat sheet. Ask: "Make a one-page cheat sheet for a new [game name] player. Include the most important rules, common terms, and three beginner tips. Format it so I can print it."

Project 2: Build a character or team

I am building a character for [game]. I like [playstyle, e.g., healing, sneaking, hard-hitting]. Suggest three beginner-friendly builds. For each, list the key stats, gear, or abilities to focus on, and explain why in one sentence.

Then:

Write a checklist of what to do in my first five sessions with this character.

Project 3: Plan game night or a raid

I am hosting a game night for four people who have never played [game]. Make a two-hour schedule: setup, teaching the rules, first round, breaks, and cleanup. Include a snack list and a "things to print" list.

For online games:

I play [game] with a regular group. Suggest a simple way to track our session notes (who played what, loot, decisions) using a free notes app or spreadsheet.

Project 4: FFXIV-specific ideas

Final Fantasy XIV is a big online game with lots to track. AI can help with:

  • Understanding the story: "I am new to FFXIV. Explain the main story arc in plain English, without spoilers past [expansion]."
  • Class and job advice: "I want to play a healer in FFXIV. Compare the beginner-friendly healer jobs and tell me which is easiest to learn."
  • Gear and progression: "I just reached level 50 in FFXIV. What should I do next in plain steps?"
  • Rotation ideas: "Explain what a rotation is in FFXIV, using a tank job as an example."

Privacy note for FFXIV: Do not paste your Lodestone ID, character name if it uses your real name, server, or FC (free company) details. Keep it general: "I play a tank on a North American data center."

Project 5: Keep a game journal

Suggest a simple game journal template I can use in a notebook or notes app. Include space for: date, game, session goal, what happened, next steps, and a rating out of 5.

Print one per session or copy it into a document.

Tips

  • AI does not know last week's patch or update unless you tell it. Double-check stats against the game's official wiki.
  • AI can make up items, quests, or recipes. Treat its game facts as a starting point, not the final word.
  • For multiplayer games, never share your password or account recovery info with an AI — or with anyone else.
  • Take breaks. AI can plan your game time, but you decide when to stop.
