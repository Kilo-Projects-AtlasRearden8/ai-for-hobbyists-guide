KDP & Book Publishing

Using AI to help with Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) and self-publishing. Written for authors and hobbyists — not a guarantee of sales or income.

Remember: Never paste your manuscript, personal address, tax ID, or banking info into an AI tool. See Privacy and Safety.

How AI can help

  • Brainstorming book titles and subtitles
  • Creating a chapter outline from your idea
  • Writing a book description or blurb
  • Suggesting keywords and categories

Enter setup and payment info only on Amazon's official site, never into an AI chat.
