Reselling with AI

Use free AI to help with reselling items — books, craft supplies, collectibles, hobby gear — on eBay, Amazon, or local platforms.

Remember: Never paste customer names, addresses, order details, or payment info into an AI tool. See Privacy and Safety.

Example: writing a listing

I am selling a used [item] in [general condition]. Write a short, honest eBay title and description. Include what it is, any flaws, and what comes with it. Use placeholders like [YOUR CITY] instead of my real location.
