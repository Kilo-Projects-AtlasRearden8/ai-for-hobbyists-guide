Bookkeeping Basics

Use free AI to understand small-business and hobby bookkeeping. Educational, not accounting advice. For tax filing, talk to a qualified accountant.

Remember: Never paste real bank statements, tax IDs, customer payment info, or your Social Security number into an AI tool. See Privacy and Safety.

AI can help with

  • Explaining bookkeeping terms in plain English
  • Suggesting categories for income and expenses
  • Creating a simple spreadsheet layout for tracking sales
  • Drafting a simple invoice template
