Low-Energy Productivity

Use free AI to make everyday tasks easier when you have low energy or executive-function challenges. AI is a helper, not a cure.

Remember: Never paste personal info, health details, or anyone else's private information into an AI tool. See Privacy and Safety.

The "too big to start" problem

Ask:

Break [task] into steps so small each one takes under five minutes. List them in order. Keep the tone kind and encouraging.
