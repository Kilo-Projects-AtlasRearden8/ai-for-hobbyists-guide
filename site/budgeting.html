Budgeting

Use free AI to build a simple budget. Educational, not financial advice. For major money decisions, talk to a qualified financial professional.

Remember: Never paste real bank account numbers, credit card numbers, or exact transaction details into an AI tool. See Privacy and Safety.

A simple budget to start

  1. List your monthly income.
  2. List your monthly needs: rent, food, utilities, transportation.
  3. List your wants: hobbies, entertainment, dining out.
  4. Subtract needs from income. What is left is for wants and savings.
